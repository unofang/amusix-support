# AMusiX Support

AMusiX keeps one song list across Apple Music, QQ Music, NetEase Cloud Music
and Spotify. Apple Music songs play inside AMusiX; songs from other services
open in their own app.

## Common questions

**Why doesn't an Apple Music song play?**
AMusiX needs Apple Music access (Settings → AMusiX → Media & Apple Music) and an
Apple Music subscription.

**Why does a song open another app?**
That song isn't on Apple Music, or you chose to play it in the app you added it
from. That app has to be installed.

**Why do I need to tap to continue when my iPhone is locked?**
iOS doesn't let one app open another from the lock screen. Tap the AMusiX card
on the lock screen to go on to the next song.

**Why is the next song a few seconds early or late?**
AMusiX can't see playback inside other apps, so it times each song by its
length. Pausing or skipping in the other app moves the real end.

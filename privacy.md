# AMusiX Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

AMusiX does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts,
no analytics, no advertising and no server.

## What stays on your iPhone

- **Your song list and queue.** The songs and links you add, and where playback
  is up to, are saved on your iPhone only. Deleting AMusiX deletes them.
- **Apple Music.** With your permission, AMusiX uses Apple's MusicKit to search
  Apple Music and play songs. Apple handles your Apple Music account under
  Apple's own privacy policy; AMusiX never sees your Apple ID.
- **Notifications and the lock-screen card.** "Next song" reminders and the
  Live Activity are created on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us.

## When AMusiX contacts other services

- **Song details.** When you add a song link from QQ Music, NetEase Cloud Music
  or Spotify, AMusiX opens that one page once, in a private web view on your
  iPhone, to read the song's title, artist and length that the page publishes.
  That service sees an ordinary page visit. Nothing else is read or kept.
- **Opening songs.** When a song plays in another app, AMusiX asks iOS to open
  that song's link in that app. What happens there is covered by that app's
  privacy policy.

## Children

AMusiX collects no data from anyone, including children.
